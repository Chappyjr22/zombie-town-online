Three very different problems

Town Night

A burning fountain in a dead crossroads. Five buildings, a bank vault, a church tower, and a bar with an upstairs balcony you can reach by jump pad.

Nuketown Daylight

Trinity Ave, in a bomb test range. Two two-storey houses either side, a school bus and a moving van parked in the middle of the road as cover, three ways up to each bedroom, alleys behind the garages, back yard gardens and a lot of mannequins.

Blacksire Storm

A classified military research compound split between a rain-lashed checkpoint and the containment lab below. Loop through barracks, garage, communications, generator rooms, test cells, and a lockdown zone that gets meaner every fourth round.

How to play

Survive. A round arrives every two and a half minutes whether or not you cleared the last one, and each round brings two more of them than the last. Press the call button when a round is clear if you would rather not wait.

Earning

Ten points a hit, fifty a kill, ninety for a headshot. Later rounds multiply all of it, so a clear on round twenty is worth roughly eighteen times a clear on round one.

Spending

Chalk outlines on walls are weapons, and buying one you already own refills its ammo at half price. Vending machines are perks. Follow the beam of light to the mystery box: nine hundred and fifty a roll, and it will not hand you something you are already carrying. Pack-a-Punch your weapon for five thousand, then fully upgrade it for fifteen thousand.

Staying alive

Juggernog takes you to two hundred and fifty health. Quick Revive gets you back up once, with five seconds of invulnerability to fight your way clear. Keep moving, use the buildings, and remember the bar has an upstairs.

Drops

Zombies sometimes drop Max Ammo, Insta-Kill, Double Points, a Nuke or a Fire Sale. Walk over them. Every sixtieth kill is guaranteed to leave something.
